Custom dock icons, drawn exactly as designed (no tile or conversion added).

Drop a square PNG here (1024x1024 is ideal) named after the app as its dock label shows it,
e.g. 'Discord.png', 'Spotify.png', 'Steam.png', or after its exe, e.g. 'WindowsTerminal.png'.
A "<name>.glyph.png" is used as the source for the normal silver glyph on a dark tile (same size and box as other icons).
Special names: 'finder.png' and 'trash.png'.

macOS Tahoe-style icons: https://macosicons.com (search the app, download the PNG).
Restart Liquid after adding icons (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Q, then start it again).
